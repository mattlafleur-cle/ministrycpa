Speaking & Workshops

Most rooms full of pastors, administrators, and board members have never had ministry finance explained in plain English by someone who actually does this work. Matt LaFleur is a CPA who spends his days inside church and nonprofit books, and his talks turn the topics people dread into the ones they quote on the way out. Available for conferences, denominational gatherings, association events, staff trainings, and board retreats.

By Matt LaFleur, CPA

Signature Talks

Six sessions built for ministry finance audiences

01

What Your Church Is Probably Getting Wrong About Ministry Money

The Flagship Session

The five most common ministry finance failures - housing allowance, dual tax status, restricted funds, 990 readiness, and internal controls - what each one actually costs, and who in the room is positioned to catch them.

02

The Housing Allowance, Done Right

Administrators, Treasurers & Executive Pastors

Designation timing, the three-part test, substantiation, and the board's role in getting it right before December, not after.

03

Restricted Funds Are a Legal Promise

Board-Facing Session

When a donor restricts a gift, that restriction is not a suggestion. Fund accounting, donor trust, and the liability most boards do not know they are carrying.

04

Internal Controls That Fit a Small Staff

Practical, Not Theoretical

No segregation-of-duties fantasy. Controls a two- or three-person church office can actually run on a Monday morning, without anyone feeling accused.

05

The 990 Before It Is Due

Nonprofit Finance Leaders

A readiness session: what the form actually asks, what reviewers and donors look for, and how to make filing season boring.

06

AI for Ministry Finance Operations

Where It Helps, and Where It Must Not Touch

Where AI genuinely helps a church or nonprofit finance office, and where it must not touch client data, donor records, or judgment calls. Start with the work, not the tool.

Every talk is built for the room it's given in. Denominations, associations, and ECFA member events can request tailored versions.

About Matt

Matt LaFleur is a Cleveland-based CPA, the founder of Forest City CPA, and the creator of The Ministry CPA and its weekly newsletter, Called to Account. He works directly with executive pastors, church administrators, treasurers, and nonprofit finance leaders on the problems general accounting firms tend to miss: housing allowance elections, restricted fund tracking, 990 readiness, and the internal controls that keep a ministry's finances defensible.
